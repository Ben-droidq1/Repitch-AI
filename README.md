## Repitch AI
One idea. Every platform. Zero reshaping.
Paste a YouTube transcript, blog post, or any text. Repitch generates tweets, a LinkedIn post, a newsletter, a video script, and an SEO blog all at once.

## What It Does
📱 Tweets — hook-first, under 280 characters

💼 LinkedIn Post — thought-leadership angle

📧 Newsletter — subject line + 3 insights

🎬 Video Script — 60–90s format with B-roll cues

📝 SEO Blog — H2/H3 structure, optimized

Pick your tone (Professional, Casual, Witty, Educational, Hype/Web3) and generate all formats simultaneously.

## Use Cases
Content Creators — Turn one YouTube video into a full week of posts across all platforms

Indie Hackers — Ship a product launch across Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletters in minutes

Web3 Builders — Amplify announcements, governance updates, and thread drops instantly

Thought Leaders — Repurpose speaking engagements and insights across 6 channels

Newsletter Writers — Transform subscriber stories into viral tweets and LinkedIn posts

Marketers — Generate 30-day campaigns from a single piece of source material
